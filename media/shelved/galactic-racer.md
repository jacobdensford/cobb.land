To quote [myself quoting myself](/posts/reddit-post-collection/):

> As someone who still loves and occasionally plays the episode one podracer game, this ain't it. It's pretty fun, but it's a fundamentally different kind if game.

That was my take after a few hours. My opinion after having beat the game is similar. This game is not the successor to Star Wars Episode I: Racer. It's its own thing. And what it is is pretty good! It has an addictive and fun, but very frustrating at times, gameplay loop—it's a rougelike, after all. I would rather be able to play a repeatable and predictable series of races and slowly but surely upgrade my vehicle. You can't do that in this game, really. But you can steer towards (pun intended) a particular build and specific kinds of races. In the end, that was good enough for me. **I recommend it.**
