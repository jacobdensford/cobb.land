I don't have much context for NES games. I started with the SNES. Maybe all of those old games are insanely difficult. Even many SNES games were pretty hard. But *Malasombra* feels like it's on another level. At first, I tried playing it on original hardware, but after it took me an hour just to reach the first save point, I switched to emulation so I could use save states. The game looks great and has some good gameplay, and if it was just a little easier it would be easy to recommend, but the requirement for precision is just too much for me. Maybe it won't be for you. **I recommend it with reservations.**
