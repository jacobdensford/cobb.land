I wanted to enjoy *Mr. Penumbra's 24-Hour Bookstore*, and I even did sometimes. But, mostly, it was just too early 2010s. 

Let me explain.

Do you remember when tech was cool, when a job in Silicon Valley was a dream job, and when Google's motto was "Don't be evil" and we believed them? This book solidly lives in that time with those ideas. To me, in this age of enshittification, that makes it pretty unpalatable. (Not to mention, as always, Robin Sloan loves the bay area waaaay too much.)

I genuinely enjoyed most of this book—the twists and turns, the mystery, the history. I liked the idea of the book. But I just couldn't get past the vibe. But maybe you can. So **I recommend it with reservations.**
